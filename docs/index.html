How it works

One operating cycle, the same for every client.

Every client gets its own folder, its own data history and its own schedule. The cycle below runs in this order the first time, then the loops keep it going.

  1. 1 · Onboard

    Business, platform, competitors

    Business facts come from the owner, never guessed. The system finds the 3–5 biggest competitors with free authority numbers and drafts 20–50 real customer questions.

  2. 2 · Baseline

    Where the site stands today

    90 days of Search Console and GA4, the index status of every URL, mobile speed and conversion tracking, saved as a snapshot to compare against.

  3. 3 · Audit and fix

    Changes you can review

    Technical and AI-readiness audits. Fixes are made on a separate branch, built, checked, and shown on a preview link before anyone merges.

  4. 4 · Content

    Pages that answer real questions

    Gaps come from AI answers and Search Console. Every guide starts from a sourced fact sheet; numbers are checked again after translation.

  5. 5 · AI visibility

    Are you cited by AI search?

    Every customer question is asked to AI search. The system records who gets cited, where the answers are weak, and which pages to build.

  6. 6 · Loops

    It keeps running

    A Monday report, a monthly technical check and a monthly AI round compare each period with the last and say what to do next.

Ground rules

Built-in rules that keep the work honest.

  • Free tools onlyGoogle Search Console, GA4, PageSpeed, CrUX, Bing Webmaster and open data. Anything that needs a paid tool is skipped and named.
  • Facts come from the ownerUnknown business details stay "TBD". Every number names its source and date.
  • Platform-specific adviceFixes are written for the client's own platform: custom code, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or others.
  • Nothing ships unreviewedCode changes go through a branch and a preview; the owner merges. Outreach is drafts only.
  • Honest statusPlanned features are labelled planned. Early results are labelled early.
  • Everything is loggedEach client has a dated work log, data snapshots and a versioned backup.

Limits

What it does not do yet.

  • Keyword search volumesFree sources only, so topics are chosen from Search Console, customer questions and AI answers rather than volume estimates.
  • Scheduled work needs a running computerLoops run from the operator's machine today; moving them to the cloud is on the roadmap.
  • AI tracking is a monthly sampleThe same questions every month give a clear direction, not an exact share of voice.
  • Authority takes the owner's timeReviews, business profiles and outreach need the owner to act; the system drafts and tracks.
